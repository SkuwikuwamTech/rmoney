THE SKUWIKUWAM TECHNOLOGIES PHILOSOPHY

Building the Counterweight to
Corporate Cloud Enclosure.

Modern tech companies turned software into endless rent and treated users' personal data as an unconsented commodity. We founded Skuwikuwam Technologies to prove there is a better, more respectful way.

Why We Founded Skuwikuwam

Over the last decade, personal computing suffered a quiet tragedy: almost every essential everyday tool was coerced into the cloud. Simple budgeting spreadsheets were replaced with $120/year SaaS apps that scrape bank logins, inject tracking pixels into user dashboards, and sell aggregated consumer spending habits to advertising brokers.

If your internet goes out, your software stops working. If the company changes its terms or goes out of business, your historical records vanish. If their servers are breached, your financial ledger is published on the dark web.

"Software should be a tool that serves its owner — not a surveillance trap or an endless recurring landlord."

At Skuwikuwam Technologies, we design and ship software built on the principles of digital sovereignty: tools that compile to fast native binaries, write to transparent local files on your machine, work without internet, and never expire.

Our Guiding North Star

The Skuwikuwam Manifesto

01

Local-First Storage

Your computer has a multi-terabyte SSD. There is no legitimate technical reason for a personal financial app to store your ledger on a remote cloud server. Skuwikuwam apps store 100% of state locally in open SQLite databases that you own and control completely.

02

Zero Telemetry & Tracking

We do not log what transactions you record, what categories you create, or when you open the software. We do not embed Mixpanel, Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, or telemetry phone-home daemons. Our apps are built to execute silently.

03

Perpetual Lifetime Ownership

We despise subscription fatigue. Software tools should be bought once and owned forever, just like a hammer or a piano. When you purchase rMoney Pro, your license is perpetual, offline-verifiable, and includes all future updates without monthly recurring fees.

04

No Proprietary Lock-In

If you ever decide to stop using a Skuwikuwam application, your data is never trapped in an obfuscated binary format. Every table is standard SQLite, with 1-click CSV, JSON, and PDF audit statement exports built directly into the interface.

Engineering Standards

Built on Rust & Tauri v2

0 MB Cloud servers hosting your personal financial records
100% Offline functionality across Windows, macOS, and Linux
AES-256 End-to-end device sync encryption standards

By utilizing Tauri v2, our applications leverage the native OS webview and a memory-safe Rust backend instead of bundling heavy, vulnerable Electron runtimes. This yields cold launch times under 400 milliseconds, memory consumption around 60MB, and a lightweight installer under 5MB.
